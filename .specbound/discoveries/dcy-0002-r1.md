Treating the Micro-SPEC/QC Discovery as authority for this different control-plane work would make lifecycle lineage misleading.
- **Target users / affected systems:** Repository maintainers and delivery agents who need to create, review, revise, and validate canonical REQs without overwriting history or inferring authority from Markdown status, a CLI invocation, or tracker state.
- **Current state and evidence:** `req-0001-r1` documents the desired REQ-control-plane slice but records its parent-scope mismatch as a blocker. `dcy-0001-r1` and its confirmation authorize only REQ drafting for the Micro-SPEC/QC evidence topic. The repository-local skill documents that confirmed Discoveries are content-addressed and authorize only `draft_req_only`.
