## 12. Recommendation

- **Recommended direction:** Create separate canonical artifacts for Micro-SPEC, iteration QC, and delivery QC, each with a single factual owner and a narrow transition-specific binding; retain merge/release authority outside this slice.
- **Why this direction:** It matches the existing lifecycle model, prevents partial iteration evidence from being mistaken for complete delivery approval, and keeps a future validator fail-closed without mutating approved REQs.
- **Trade-offs accepted or deferred:** More artifacts and schema complexity are accepted. Exact paths, schemas, record-by-record binding matrix, and evidence representation remain REQ design work; they must implement the selected policy without expanding it.
