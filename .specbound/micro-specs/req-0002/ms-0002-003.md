# MS-0002-003 — REQ revision policy and approval binding

## 1. Objective

Satisfy **REQ-0002-r1 AC-004 and AC-005** as one coupled implementation slice: approval currentness cannot be fail-closed without the configured REQ revision policy that defines it.

## 2. Selected scope

- `specbound.yaml` declares `requirement_revision_policy: latest_only_with_explicit_exception`.
- `preflight` rejects absent, malformed, or different revision-policy configuration.
- Validation detects duplicate canonical `(requirement_id, revision)` identities without treating a newer revision as deletion of old history.
- For an approved non-latest revision, validation blocks by default. It accepts only a structured `supersession_exception` with exactly a substantive reason, matching approval authority, and timezone-bearing ISO-8601 timestamp.
- Existing approval binding remains fail-closed for canonical path, ID, revision, digest, risk, and authority. Invalid approval evidence must not increment `approved_requirements`.
- Focused tests cover the default block, valid exception, malformed exception, and approval digest mismatch.

## 3. Non-goals and boundary

- No REQ approval command or automatic approval-record issuance.
- No mutation of an approved REQ to repair its digest; a material revision remains a new numeric artifact.
- No migration policy for external/pre-existing adopter repositories.
- No claim that unrelated discovery-confirmation or rejection behavior is part of this AC slice; preserve unrelated working-tree changes.

## 4. Acceptance checks

1. Preflight recognizes only the explicit configured policy value.
2. A newer `req-<id>-rN` causes an older approved revision to be blocked by default, not deleted or silently invalidated.
3. A valid authority-bound exception permits historical approval validation; malformed timestamp, authority mismatch, placeholder/empty reason, or unexpected shape is rejected.
4. Missing/malformed/mismatching canonical approval evidence leaves the approved-REQ counter unchanged.

## 5. Bounded verification plan
